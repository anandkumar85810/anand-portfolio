About Me

Professional Journey & Background

I am Anand Kumar, a dedicated Full Stack Developer with hands-on professional experience in PHP and Laravel backend development.

I completed my BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) in 2023. I launched my software development career as a PHP Laravel Developer Intern at Deytal Technologies Pvt. Ltd. on 19 February, successfully completing a 3-month internship program.

Following my internship performance, I transitioned into a Full-time Full Stack Developer role at Deytal Technologies Pvt. Ltd. in Noida, India.

My primary stack includes PHP, Laravel, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap. In addition, I have hands-on knowledge and project experience in Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, and Hibernate/JPA. I enjoy building practical web applications, designing efficient database schemas, and writing clean backend code to solve real-world problems.
